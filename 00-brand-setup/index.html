From template to running business

Your milestones for each brand, from duplicating the master template to launch and day-to-day operation. The detailed checklists sit in the step pages linked inside each milestone.

Once, before the first brand: after BQ hands over the master (step 1).

References: Distribution strategy map

1Brand site setupTurn the template into the brand’s website.
  • Duplicate the master and apply the brand (theme, logo, name, domain, email) — step 2
  • Switch off modules the brand doesn’t need — step 3
  • Legal pages and cookie banner for the brand
2Positioning and offerDecide who it’s for and what you sell first.
  • Target customer and the problem in their words
  • Value hypothesis: complaint, promise, action
  • Signature framework and core message
  • First offer and the free lead magnet
3Pricing strategySet what each offer costs and how people move up.
  • Price ladder: free → low-cost → core → premium
  • Plan names, prices and benefits; one-off product prices
  • Payment plans, member discounts, launch offer
  • VAT and KOR decision with the accountant
4Website contentReplace every placeholder with real content.
  • Core pages, offers and the modules you kept — step 4
  • Start-here articles and featured posts
  • Free guide and welcome email sequence
5Social media presenceBe findable where the audience already is.
  • Plan channels with the distribution strategy map
  • Pick 1–2 platforms that fit the audience
  • Profiles with brand, bio and link to the free guide
  • Content pillars and a posting rhythm
  • Repurpose articles into posts; reply and engage
6Validation and pre-launchProve demand before spending more.
  • Waitlist live for the first offer
  • Target customer, watering holes, message and motion (see the distribution strategy map)
  • Conversations with strangers in the target group
  • Validation log: engagement, direct requests, pre-pay willingness
  • Pre-sales or early-access offer
7Commerce, tax and AI-readinessMake sure people can pay and AI tools can find you.
  • Payments, checkout, VAT, bookkeeping, test purchases — step 5
  • AI-ready per-brand steps — step 6
8LaunchOpen the doors.
  • Launch approach and order of pages — step 7
  • Announce to waitlist, network and social
  • First customers delivered personally; collect feedback and testimonials
9Operations and working rhythmRun the business alongside the job without dropping balls.
  • Weekly rhythm: content, outreach, delivery, admin
  • Simple systems: lead capture, delivery, finance tracking
  • Time and money budget per brand
  • Automate only steps done by hand three times or more
10Measure and growLearn what works and do more of it.
  • Monthly and quarterly checks — step 8
  • Track signups, conversion, revenue, retention; unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback) from the distribution strategy map
  • Adjust offers and prices on evidence
  • Grow reach: partnerships, speaking, referrals; add modules when there is demand
