How is the template transferred to my Wix Studio account?

Once purchased, we trigger a full Wix Studio Site Transfer directly to your email address. You accept the transfer and instantly become the 100% owner of the site inside your own Wix Studio workspace.

Do I need coding skills to customize or manage this site?

No coding required whatsoever. Everything relies on native Wix Studio apps (Stores, Bookings, Events, Online Programs, Pricing Plans). Day-to-day edits can even be done using a content-only role so design layouts are never accidentally broken.

Can I hide modules I don't need right now (e.g., Shop or Events)?

Yes! Every module (Courses, Shop, Bookings, Events) is independently hideable. You can hide any page from site menus with two clicks without breaking any of the remaining sections.

What Wix Plan is required to run this template?

You will need a standard Wix Studio Business plan that supports payments, online bookings, and course subscriptions. We provide a full plan recommendation guide upon transfer.
