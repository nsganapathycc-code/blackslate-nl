One question, three passes, no shortcuts.

Most advice starts from what everyone already believes. This method starts from what you can check, and keeps going until the weak spots show.

1

Strip it down

Your question is split into the smallest claims it depends on. Each one is marked as known, measured or assumed.

2

Rebuild it

The answer is reassembled only from the claims that hold, so you can see exactly what it rests on.

3

Let the critics in

Separate critic passes attack the logic, the numbers and the blind spots. What survives goes in your report.
